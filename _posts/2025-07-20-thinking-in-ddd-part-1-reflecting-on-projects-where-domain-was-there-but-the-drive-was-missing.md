Over the past few years, although the companies I worked for in New Zealand were in different industries, the project pain points I encountered as a developer were remarkably similar. Each company had a small team of just three developers, maintaining products that had been running for around ten years. The core business was made up of two to three sub-products, forming a complete ecosystem. While the technology stacks varied slightly, they were all based on ASP.NET MVC and React frameworks. I believe this is a common project setup for many small companies in New Zealand, and the pain points I observed may have broader relevance.

## Technical Debt Everywhere
